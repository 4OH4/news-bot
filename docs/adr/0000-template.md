## Context

What problem or question prompted this decision? Describe the forces at play
(requirements, constraints, risks) and link the relevant `PROJECT_PLAN.md`
sections or issues. Stay factual and neutral.

## Decision

What we will do, stated plainly ("We will ..."). Include enough detail for
someone to act on it without re-reading the discussion. Briefly note the main
alternatives considered and why they were not chosen.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. List positive and negative
consequences, follow-up work this creates, and the conditions under which the
decision should be revisited.
